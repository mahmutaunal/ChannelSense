# ChannelSense Privacy Policy

ChannelSense performs Wi-Fi analysis locally on the device.

- No account is required.
- No analytics, advertising SDK, or remote backend is included.
- Wi-Fi scan results, SSIDs, BSSIDs, and location data are not transmitted.
- Router credentials are never requested or stored.
- Permissions are used only to access Android Wi-Fi scan information and connected-network context.

Android classifies Wi-Fi scanning as location-sensitive because nearby networks can sometimes be used to infer location. ChannelSense does not derive, save, or share physical location.
